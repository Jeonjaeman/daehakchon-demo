Gammak Highball: Persimmon Vinegar Fiber + Makgeolli

The Gammak Highball is drawing attention as a new way to drink.

On social media and online communities, a recipe called the "Gammak Highball",
which combines Persimmon Vinegar Fiber and makgeolli, is getting attention.

The fresh flavor of Persimmon Vinegar Fiber blends with the smooth taste of makgeolli for a distinctive appeal,
and it tastes even cleaner served cold.

Recommended ratio
✔ Persimmon Vinegar Fiber: 1~2 bottles
✔ Makgeolli: 1 bottle

Adjust the ratio to your taste and enjoy your own Gammak Highball.

※ This recipe is an example of the ways consumers enjoy the drink. Taste and ratio may vary with personal preference.
