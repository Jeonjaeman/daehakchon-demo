OEM SERVICE

From Planning to Delivery,
The Foundation of Your Success

From health functional foods to red ginseng, vitamin and hangover relief drinks. We make our partners' products with university R&D and 30 years of production experience.

  1. STEP 01Consulting · Planning

    Together we define the product concept, ingredients, container and packaging.

  2. STEP 02Formulation

    We develop the formulation and build prototypes with the university food research institute.

  3. STEP 03Quality Verification

    In-house analysis and trial runs finalize taste and specifications.

  4. STEP 04Mass Production

    Produced in bottles, PET and pouches on GMP and HACCP lines.

  5. STEP 05Packing · Delivery

    Separately packed and palletized, then delivered on schedule.
