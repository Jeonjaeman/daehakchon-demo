PRODUCTION PROCESS

From Ingredients to Delivery,
A Nine-Step Process

As an MFDS GMP-certified manufacturer, we manage every step from raw material supply to delivery. Scroll to follow the process step by step.

  1. 01Raw Material SupplyInspected ingredients are fed into the mixing tank.
  2. 02Container SupplyBottle, PET and pouch containers are fed onto the line.
  3. 03WashingContainers are cleaned in the bottle washer.
  4. 04FillingPrecision fillers fill each container.
  5. 05SealingCappers and sealers seal each container.
  6. 06Post-Sterilization and CoolingPost-sterilization and cooling stabilize quality.
  7. 07InspectionChecked for foreign matter, fill volume and seal integrity.
  8. 08Packaging and PalletizingLabeled, case-packed and stacked on pallets.
  9. 09DeliveryShipped to our partners.
