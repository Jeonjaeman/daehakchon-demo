About UsCI&BIHistoryLocation

Since its founding in 1996, Kyongbuk Science University Food Factory has commercialized traditional foods such as persimmon vinegar based on university research, selling them through its own channels and supplying major food companies. As a school enterprise that draws on the expertise and research capabilities of food-related departments to bring science to traditional food ingredients and to research, develop and manufacture health functional foods, it has been designated a GMP-certified health functional food manufacturer by the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS). It is also run as a nonprofit educational enterprise, and all profits are returned to students as scholarships.
